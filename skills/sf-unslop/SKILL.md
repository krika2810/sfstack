# sf-unslop

Remove AI tells from writing: inflated words ("delve", "leverage", "robust", "seamless", "comprehensive"), filler openers ("I'd be happy to", "Great question"), formulaic triads, synonym cycling, hollow emphasis. Say the plain thing in the plain words and stop when done. Applies to docs, PR bodies, commit messages, and chat replies alike.
